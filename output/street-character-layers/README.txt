Dreamio — Leather & Lace

Six transparent SVG item cutouts: jacket, dress, boots, bag, headband, earrings.
Each embeds the generated PNG pixels and retains the shared 887 x 1774 canvas.
Use the fitting-room renderer for complete outfit changes: the clean jacket and sleeveless dress variants provide occluded pixels.
The lace dress and original boots stay equipped; jacket and accessories can be removed, and the dress has three color choices.
Original photos are not included.
Regenerate exports: node --import tsx scripts/export-street-character.ts
